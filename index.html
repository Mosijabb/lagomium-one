The idea

Lagom is a measurement, not a mood.

What we measure

Too little

No booking link, thin service pages, missing business profile, nothing to build trust. Customers arrive, find no answer, and leave.

Just right

Loads instantly, says what you do and what it costs, one obvious way to book, and it appears when someone nearby searches. That is the whole job.

Too much

Forty plugins, three chat widgets, a sliding banner nobody reads, and a five-second load. You are paying for weight that costs you customers.

Questions

The things people ask first

How long does a new site take?

Two to three weeks from the day your content is ready. The delay is almost always photos and text, so we help you get those sorted first.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain, the content and the code are yours. If you ever leave, we hand everything over and help you move it.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Care and Growth are month to month. Cancel whenever you like and the site stays yours.

Can you work with the site I already have?

Usually, yes. If your WordPress site has good bones we improve and maintain it rather than charge you for a rebuild you don't need.

What exactly is the AI assistant?

A chat widget trained on your services, prices and opening hours. It answers routine questions around the clock and passes real enquiries to you with the details already collected.

Do you work in Swedish and English?

Both. Sites can be built bilingual if your customers need it.
